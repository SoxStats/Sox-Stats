How We Do This

Every cutoff on this site is a choice. Here's what we chose and why, so you can tell us we're wrong with the facts in hand.

Player tiers

We judge a career by its best three seasons with 300 or more plate appearances. A season he didn't play counts as zero. He gets the higher of his two tiers, one from WAR and one from wRC+.

The stats we use

WAR (wins above replacement) is how many wins a player is worth compared with a guy you could call up from AAA. We use Baseball-Reference's version.

wRC+ measures a hitter's total offense. 100 is league average; 120 is 20% better. We calculate it ourselves from MLB's official stats, adjusted for the league and year but not for ballpark.

Where the numbers come from

MLB's official stats for every season since 1876 and every minor league season since 2005, plus WAR from Baseball-Reference.

Borderline cases

When a cutoff could reasonably go either way, the article says so and shows what changes if you move it.

We don't delete old takes

If we were wrong, the original stays up with a dated update underneath.
