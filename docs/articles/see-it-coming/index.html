How we did this

    Every winter, Sox fans (me included) look at a prospect's minor league numbers and try to guess what he'll become. Some calls look obvious after the fact. Of course Mike Trout was going to be Mike Trout.

    But how much can the numbers actually tell you? I took every hitter who reached AA from 2005 to 2018, looked at what he did there, and checked what he turned into.

    My guess going in

    The young guys who rake will mostly make it. The ones who'll burn you are the young guys who have a great year but never fix the strikeouts.
