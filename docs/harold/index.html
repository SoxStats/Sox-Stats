What's a Harold?

A Harold is one Harold Baines career: about 38.8 WAR. It's our unit for a very good career.

Baines played 22 seasons and made the Hall of Fame. Plenty of people think he shouldn't be in. We think he's the perfect measuring stick for a guy who was really good for a really long time.

The scale

Career WARWhat we call it
70 or moreHall of Fame lock
About 38 to 70Hall of Really Good, unless he beats the typical Hall of Famer at his position, or his career was cut short (think Kirby Puckett)
