About

I'm Aaron. I'm a stat fan, not a stat guy.

This isn't FanGraphs or Baseball Prospectus, and it isn't trying to be. When I wonder about something, like whether a guy who strikes out this much can be a star, I go find out. Then I show you how I did it, so you can argue with me.

It's mostly the Sox, with some baseball in general. The arguing happens on Facebook. The charts live here.

My day job is insurance. I run an independent agency licensed in Indiana and Illinois, and I look at coverage the same way I look at baseball: find the holes.
